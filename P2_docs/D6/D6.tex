\section{Workload split}

The intended division for Part~2 was \emph{Berk = interpreter +
rendering + Part-1 parser carryover}, \emph{İsmail = type checker +
Part-1 lexer carryover}, with the cross-cutting shared modules
(\texttt{environment.py}, \texttt{runtime\_values.py},
\texttt{errors.py}) drafted by Berk and reviewed by İsmail per the
plan~\S2.5 / \S2.6 contract.

The final split is approximately \textbf{Berk 60\% / İsmail 40\%}.
Berk's contribution is heavier on documentation work in the final
days because İsmail had personal time pressure during the closing
hours of Part~2 and Berk picked up the LaTeX assembly, the D3 PDF
assembly, the D4 journal continuation, and the bulk of the D5 +
D6 drafting on behalf of the pair. İsmail's substantive contribution
is the type checker (\textasciitilde 700 lines, all 19 spec~\S10
errors implemented), the Part-1 lexer (\textasciitilde 330 lines),
plus the late-cycle bug-fix round that produced the \texttt{scale}
re-execution rule via \texttt{captured\_params} -- both modules are
load-bearing and account for substantially more than the 40\% floor
the handout sets.

Each partner's contribution is at or above the 40\% threshold the
handout requires.
